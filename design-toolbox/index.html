The Design Toolbox

Every pattern I reach for, or watch other people reach for, when designing an API, from the HTTP and TCP layers up through the business, pricing and policy decisions that sit around them. Each one has what it is, when to use it, what to watch out for, an example and the standard or reference behind it. Search, filter and click any row to open it.

Where a pattern is something APIs.io tracks across the catalog (plans, rate limits, SLAs, terms, data licenses, compliance, security, AsyncAPI, Arazzo and more), the row links to that section so you can see who actually does it. The data is available as JSON.
