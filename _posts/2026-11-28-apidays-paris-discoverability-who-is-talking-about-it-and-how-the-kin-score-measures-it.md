## How the Kin Score measures Discoverability

Discoverability measures whether an API can be found and understood from machine-readable metadata alone: APIs.json completeness, tags and identity signals.

APIs.io exists because of this facet. It's a search engine built on providers describing their own APIs in machine-readable form. The talks above show the same idea arriving inside enterprises and governments: publish metadata at the source, organize by business capability, and let catalogs and agents find what you have. I'll be talking about how Europe's government APIs look from the outside-in, which is mostly a discoverability story.
