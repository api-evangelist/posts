Over the last two and a half weeks I published one post each business day on a single way people told me they want to use the [Kin Score](https://apis.io/rating/) and [APIs.io](https://apis.io/). They all came from listening to people who run API programs talk it through for themselves. Twelve uses came up that I wanted to write about, and here they all are in one place.

## Inside your own organization

- **[Use A Score To Build Your API Roadmap And Not To Chase A Number](https://apievangelist.com/2026/11/02/use-a-score-to-build-your-api-roadmap-and-not-to-chase-a-number/).** Reading the facet breakdown to pick hotspots, and deciding which low scores to accept on purpose.
- **[Make The Case For API Investment With Facts Instead Of Feelings](https://apievangelist.com/2026/11/03/make-the-case-for-api-investment-with-facts-instead-of-feelings/).** Using the gap between the Kin Score and Agent Readiness to argue for foundations while the money goes to AI.
- **[What To Check Before You Call Your API Production Ready](https://apievangelist.com/2026/11/04/what-to-check-before-you-call-your-api-production-ready/).** Security, rate limits, idempotency and error handling as the order of work for a young API.
- **[Score The APIs Nobody Outside Your Company Can See](https://apievangelist.com/2026/11/05/score-the-apis-nobody-outside-your-company-can-see/).** Finding, profiling and scoring internal APIs with a model you host, without anything leaving your network.
- **[Where A Score Fits In Your CI Pipeline And Your Developer Portal](https://apievangelist.com/2026/11/06/where-a-score-fits-in-your-ci-pipeline-and-your-developer-portal/).** Why I lean towards quarterly goals with Spectral in the pipeline, and how the score could sit in Backstage.

## Against other companies

- **[Benchmark Your APIs Against Competitors You Did Not Know Were Looking](https://apievangelist.com/2026/11/09/benchmark-your-apis-against-competitors-you-did-not-know-were-looking/).** Comparing facets with competitors, because two matching composites can hide very different operations.
- **[Scan A Whole Sector Before You Trust Your Sense Of It](https://apievangelist.com/2026/11/10/scan-a-whole-sector-before-you-trust-your-sense-of-it/).** Band counts across banking and accounting, and whether the weights should vary by industry.
- **[A Quick Read On Engineering Maturity From The Outside](https://apievangelist.com/2026/11/11/a-quick-read-on-engineering-maturity-from-the-outside/).** What the public surface suggests about how a vendor builds software, and what it cannot tell you.

## Deals and buying

- **[What An Acquisition Target Claims And What The Score Says](https://apievangelist.com/2026/11/12/what-an-acquisition-target-claims-and-what-the-score-says/).** Comparing what a target claims with what is published, and how estates look after the deal.
- **[What To Look For In A Vendor Before You Sign Or Renew](https://apievangelist.com/2026/11/13/what-to-look-for-in-a-vendor-before-you-sign-or-renew/).** Procurement questions, renewal conversations and the restaurant bathroom test.
- **[Compare API Pricing And Stay Ready To Switch Providers](https://apievangelist.com/2026/11/16/compare-api-pricing-and-stay-ready-to-switch-providers/).** Reading plans as data, and what you need in place before you can move between providers.

## As a market gate

- **[Should A Score Decide Who Gets In The Door](https://apievangelist.com/2026/11/17/should-a-score-decide-who-gets-in-the-door/).** What would have to be true for a score to act like the ratings that gate cyber security vendors.

## What I took away

I built the score to help me understand the quality of public APIs. Very little of this list is about that. People want it for planning, for arguing for budget, for asking vendors better questions, and for looking inside their own walls.

The other thing I noticed is that nobody in these conversations wanted the single number. Every useful version of this works from the facets, and starts with a decision about what you do not need to be good at.

You can [look up any provider on APIs.io](https://apis.io/providers/) for free, read [every check in the rubric](https://apis.io/rating/), or pull the [open source Kin Score from GitHub](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score). If you are using it for something that is not on this list, I would really like to hear about it, and that includes telling me where it gets things wrong.
