## How the Kin Score measures Access Clarity

Access Clarity measures what it costs, what you're allowed to do and how you get in: plans, pricing, sign-up, terms, privacy, a finops mapping, trust and compliance.

I've been writing a lot about pricing in the AI age, and Paris is full of people dealing with the consequences. When an agent can spend money on your behalf, it needs to read what a call costs before it makes it. The Kin Score rewards providers who publish their plans, pricing and terms in a form a human and a machine can both read.
