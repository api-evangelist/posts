## How the Kin Score measures Contract Governance

Contract Governance measures the artifacts that constrain a contract: published rulesets, vocabulary, declared conformance and overlays. The Kin Score reads what a ruleset declares, not the result of running it, so publishing your rules is what moves the number.

This is why OpenLint is in Paris. It's an open specification and toolbox for linting API artifacts, built in public. In my look at Europe's government APIs, the top-tier separator is a published, governed contract, and national design rules only show up where they are enforced. Most public bodies don't publish a ruleset at all. A shared, open way to write and run rules is the opening, and the speakers on this list are the people who know why it matters.
