## How the Kin Score measures Regulatory Posture

Regulatory Posture is a conditional facet. It's applied only to providers in regulated industries, and it checks consent-scoped authorization, a published security and disclosure posture, terms and privacy as a legal basis, and conformance to the industry's data standard.

Europe is where this facet matters most. Banks, insurers and public bodies already answer to regulators. What the Kin Score adds is a view of whether that posture is visible from the outside, in what the API publishes, where a partner or an agent can read it before they integrate.
