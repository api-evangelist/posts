I am a systems thinker. I see things at a macro level. I shape things best at that level, and I struggle with the day-to-day micro level. API Evangelist has long been successful at convincing people I know about APIs at a mythical macro level, and it did that through a consistent drip, drip, drip of micro level API, standards, and storytelling work since 2010. I was successful because I found and aligned with the 2013 through 2016 zeitgeist, and I am looking for that same alignment inside the current market hype and noise.

The difference between 2015 and now is that back then I had the wind at my back. Investment and storytelling lined up so neatly that I believed it was truth. It wasn't. Now the investment is somewhere else, the storytelling is more automated, and [APIs are just as important as they ever were, despite being 100x more uncool to talk about](https://apievangelist.com/2026/08/16/apis-are-100x-more-important-and-still-uncool/). Traffic on API Evangelist and APIs.io is growing massively — I publish the weekly numbers for [API Evangelist](https://search.apievangelist.com/analytics/) and for [APIs.io](https://apis.io/analytics/) out in the open — which left me asking, "so what?" I could not quantify what it meant. After a lot more logging and analysis, I am getting closer to a thesis on how I need to ride the zeitgeist waves of 2026 and beyond.

Here is the breakdown of who — and *who* is doing some work in that sentence — is coming to my sites and pushing my numbers into new territory. This is measured from the APIs.io server logs, not from analytics, because analytics cannot see any of this:

| Category | Share | Who is actually at the other end |
|---|---|---|
| ANSWER ENGINE | 38.5% | A human, but they see the engine's summary, not my page. |
| DATA RESELLER | 27.3% | A paying customer of theirs, not mine. |
| CRAWL / TRAIN | 17.5% | Shaping the future, but not doing it for months. |
| USER-INITIATED | 16.8% | A human, right now, in session, looking at my brand. |

If you see the world through a classic SEO lens, you look at this and freak out, believing the value your website produces is being stolen. There is some truth in that. I have long viewed the world through that same direct-link lens, because that is what drives your Google ranking. But I have also long been comfortable with people repurposing my data and my storytelling without attribution, because it still elevates my mythical status in the API realm. That is the lens I am using on this new breakdown of who is appropriating the hard work of THE — ego emphasis fully intended — API Evangelist.

It is easy to hyperventilate about USER-INITIATED shrinking from upwards of 85% down to 16.8%. *(breathing into paper bag)* The collapse of social media referrals is a big driver of that, along with SEO shitting its pants because of Google. I know a lot of folks are shifting their SEO lens onto the CRAWL / TRAIN 17.5%. I am too. I also see a lot of people hitting the Cloudflare brakes on the DATA RESELLER 27.3%. I am not. I am welcoming it. Taking my data and reselling it is how I get more people speaking the way I speak, and in a probabilistic traffic world, that is one of the few levers you have on your own "page rank." Which brings more bots to my yard, which repeats the cycle in my favor.

You have to give away to receive in this new world. You have to let go a little to get more back. Clenching your Cloudflare butt cheeks is going to hurt you in the long run. I want to dominate and drive that ANSWER ENGINE 38.5% with my data and my storytelling, even when the attribution does not always say API Evangelist did this. Because 1) I know I did, and 2) I only have to convince the USER-INITIATED 16.8% that I did for it to become true.

This is just macro level agentic storytelling in place of the macro level human SEO and social storytelling that got me here. While people dismiss my SEO presence, keep measuring my page views with yesterday's yardstick, and get pedantic about how I use AI for artifacts and storytelling, I will be feeding this new flywheel, keeping it spinning, keeping people believing, and giving the AI answer engines what they need from the API universe.
