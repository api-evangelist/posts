## How the Kin Score measures Contract Quality

Contract Quality is 25% of the composite Kin Score, its largest facet. It measures the depth of the OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL and JSON Schema a provider actually publishes: schemas, examples, descriptions and versions.

The title of SAP's talk says it best: your AI agent is only as good as your API description. Everything the agent readiness speakers want depends on a contract that is complete and accurate. The Kin Score rewards the published contract, not the one sitting in an internal repository, because the published one is the one an agent can read.
