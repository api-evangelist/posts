As we work to build Naftiko, we are interested in talking with people about what they are facing when it comes to putting APIs to work. Throughout the summer we will be having conversations with people we know, and hopefully many people we do not know about what they face when it comes to API integration  and consumption at scale. We are looking to understand the business and technical challenges everyone is facing when it comes to the consumption of any APIs and data currently, or as part of recent work.

To help drive Naftiko market research we are looking to learn more from you about how you see the world of API consumption, framing our conversations along these aspects of the work you do putting APIs and data to work.

- What is your role working with APIs currently or historically?
- Can you share how you discover APIs as part of your work?
- Can you share how you go about integrating with APIs?
- What is involved with API consumption over time?
- How do you manage authentication across many APIs?
- What is your approach to automating with APIs?
- What business benefits do your APIs bring?

We are providing three ways in which you can take part in our research, or possibly in an ongoing way as we are working to build Naftiko open-source standards and tooling that will help you build out your approach to API integration and consumption.

- [**Survey**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1cOYE8rxcIGUbmQ8SBcPnkzQLYIXz09Jvk_6wNKWZlBo/edit) - Feel free to go ahead and submit a survey and we’ll follow up via email with any more questions, keeping the demands on your time minimal, while still making sure your voice is heard.
- **Private Interview** - Feel free to email Kin Lane via kinlane@naftiko.io and we’ll schedule a Zoom call to conduct a private recorded conversation about how you see API integrations and consumption.
- **Public Interview** - Public Interview - Feel free to email Kin Lane via kinlane@naftiko.io and we’ll schedule a Zoom call to conduct a record conversation that we’ll publish as part of an ongoing podcast series we are publishing.

Our goal with this ongoing research is to talk with API practitioners who work within companies, organizations, institutions, and government agencies about API integration and consumption. The market research of these conversations will drive the Naftiko open-source product roadmap, but you also have the opportunity for our conversation to be published as an [API Evangelist Conversation](https://conversations.apievangelist.com/) for others to learn from. Then we will be taking the highlights from these conversations and using them in a more polished and higher production podcast series that we will be distributing and promoting beginning September 2025.  

API discovery, integration, and consumption is becoming more of a priority by companies, and we would like to understand the reasons why. Naftiko is just getting started when it comes to defining our approach to contributing open-source and commercial solutions that help redefine API integration and consumption. To do this, we need your honest perspective of what is needed to make your life easier right now when it comes to managing many different APIs across many different integrations and teams. We’ll let you choose whether a quick survey submission, private conversation, or podcast conversation is right for you. We just want to learn from the realities you face in your work without bringing too many of our assumptions to the table—we look forward to hearing from you.

Feel free to email me directly at [kin@apievangelist.com](mailto:kin@apievangelist.com) with any questions, or to get started.
