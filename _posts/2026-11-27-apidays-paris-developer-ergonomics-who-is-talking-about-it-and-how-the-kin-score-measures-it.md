## How the Kin Score measures Developer Ergonomics

Developer Ergonomics measures how quickly someone can get started: SDKs, a CLI, a portal, a getting-started guide, a sandbox or console, an MCP server, and clear auth documentation.

The interesting shift in these talks is that the person getting started is now often a coding agent working for a developer. The ergonomics that help a human, like a clear getting-started guide, a working sandbox and accurate docs, turn out to be the same ones that help an agent. The Kin Score checks whether they're there.
