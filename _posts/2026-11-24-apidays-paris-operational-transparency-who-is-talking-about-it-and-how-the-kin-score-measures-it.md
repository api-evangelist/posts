## How the Kin Score measures Operational Transparency

Operational Transparency measures whether a provider publishes how its API behaves in production: rate limits, a status page, a change log, a deprecation policy and a security disclosure path.

The security talks are about defending the runtime. The Kin Score looks at what you tell your consumers about that runtime. An agent can't adjust to a rate limit it doesn't know about, or a breaking change nobody announced. When agents are calling at machine speed, publishing how your API behaves is part of defending it.
