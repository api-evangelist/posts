## Open Source Surface at APIdays Paris

Open Source Surface is a smaller facet in Paris, but it's one OpenLint cares about directly. 15 talks touch it and 6 are mostly about it: supply-chain incidents in open-source CI/CD, open collaboration in finance, and the trade-offs of moving from vendor dependency to open source.

## How the Kin Score measures Open Source Surface

Open Source Surface is a conditional facet. It's applied only where the product itself is open source, and it checks for a vulnerability disclosure path, a contribution route, a release history and a code of conduct.

OpenLint is open source, so this is the facet we hold ourselves to. The supply-chain talk from the AsyncAPI Initiative and the underfunded-standards talk are reminders that open infrastructure is only as healthy as its practices and its funding. The Kin Score reads whether those practices are published.
