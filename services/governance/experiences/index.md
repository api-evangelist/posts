        <p class="mb-3">Everything your business does eventually shows up as an experience at the intersection of a person and an interface &mdash; a developer reading your docs, a partner working through onboarding, an analyst pulling a report, an agent calling your API at three in the morning. The interface is where your operations become real to someone. Most organizations never look at it from that side.</p>
        <p class="mb-3">I help you identify the experiences that actually matter, focus on them, and refine them &mdash; the developer experience, the partner experience, the internal-team experience, and increasingly the machine experience as agents become consumers.
