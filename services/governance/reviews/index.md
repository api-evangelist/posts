        <p class="mb-3">Governance without review is just documentation. At some point someone has to look at the actual APIs, the actual policies, the actual operations, and say plainly what is working and what is not. That is the part most organizations skip, because it is uncomfortable and it takes an outside eye that is not entangled in the internal politics.</p>
        <p class="mb-3">I provide formal reviews of your policies, rules, pipelines, and skills, and of the internal, first-party, and third-party APIs running across your operations. I bring the same lens I apply to providers across <a href="https://apis.io">APIs.io</a>, and I share the feedback straight &mdash; what is strong, what is drifting, and what needs attention &mdash; in a form your teams can actually act on.</p>
        <p class="mb-3">Reviews are how governance stays honest over time, instead of decaying into a set of rules nobody checks against reality anymore.</p>
