API Evangelist services are organized into three areas of work — discovery, governance, and evangelism. Each area is made up of focused, repeatable engagements that you can mix and match to define what your business needs, operate it in consistent ways, and tell the story of the work as it happens.

Discovery

Map the words, interfaces, and standards that define what your business operations actually need — establishing the shared language and building blocks before anything gets built.

Vocabulary

Develop the words that define what is needed.

Interfaces

Map the landscape of existing commercial or open-source interfaces available, and develop new ones when needed.

Standards

Identify and develop the standards required to ensure the interoperability of any aspect of business operations.

Governance

Define and iterate upon the strategies, experiences, policies, rules, pipelines, and skills that make your business operations consistent, compliant, and repeatable.

Strategies

Define and iterate upon the business strategies that are guiding how technology is used by business operations.

Experiences

Help identify, focus on, and refine the experiences that exist at the intersection of people and interfaces.

Policies

Define and iterate upon the business policies that shape and drive your business operations in repeatable ways.

Rules

Define and iterate upon the machine-readable rules that govern the manual and automated aspects of your operations.

Pipelines

Define and iterate upon the CI/CD pipelines you need to automate the integration and deployment of operations.

Skills

Define and iterate upon the skills you need to operate your business using human and programmatic resources.

Reviews

Provide formal reviews of policies, rules, pipelines, and skills used to govern repeatable business operations.

Evangelism

Craft and publish the stories, conversations, talks, research, papers, and workshops that tell the story of your work at the intersection of people and interfaces.

Stories

Craft stories that get published to blogs, social networks, newsletters, and other channels you use for business.

Conversations

Engage in in-person and virtual conversations which can be made available via podcasts and reusable videos.

Talks

Provide in-person and virtual talks on a variety of relevant topics at the intersection of people and interfaces.

Research

Publish research into vocabulary topics, outlining interfaces, standards, policies, rules, pipelines, and skills.

Papers

Iterate and publish formal research into specific topics, providing free and commercial blueprints on topics.

Workshops

Conduct in-person and virtual workshops on relevant topics at the intersection of people and interfaces.
